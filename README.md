Duino Miner for Nintendo 3DS
A lightweight Duino Coin miner optimized for your Nintendo 3DS.
This project brings Duino Coin mining to your Nintendo 3DS, leveraging its unique capabilities. Designed for simplicity and ease of use, this miner utilizes only the bottom touchscreen for all interactions and display, leaving the top screen clear.
✨ Features
 * Bottom Screen Focused Interface: All mining statistics, settings, and controls are intuitively displayed and managed on the Nintendo 3DS's bottom touchscreen. The top screen remains blank for a clean, distraction-free experience.
 * Lightweight & Efficient: Optimized to run smoothly on the Nintendo 3DS hardware.
 * Easy Configuration: Adjust mining settings directly on the device using the touchscreen interface.
 * Real-time Stats: Monitor your mining progress and network status directly from the bottom screen.
🚀 Getting Started
Prerequisites
 * A Nintendo 3DS family console (2DS, 3DS, New 3DS, etc.) with custom firmware (CFW) installed.
 * An SD card for your 3DS.
 * A Duino Coin account (if you don't have one, visit duinocoin.com).
Installation
 * Download the latest release:
   * You can directly download the Duno-nintendo.zip file from the releases page (or use the direct download link: https://github.com/Gamming-SERVICE/Duino_miner_for_3DS/raw/main/Duno-nintendo.zip).
 * Extract the contents of Duno-nintendo.zip to the root of your 3DS's SD card.
 * Insert the SD card back into your 3DS and power it on.
 * Launch the Duino Miner application from your 3DS's Home Menu or your preferred CFW launcher (e.g., FBI).
🎮 Usage
Upon launching the application:
 * All interactions and information will appear exclusively on the bottom touchscreen.
 * Follow the on-screen prompts to enter your Duino Coin username and configure other mining settings (e.g., difficulty, threads if applicable).
 * Your mining progress, hash rate, and other relevant data will be displayed on the bottom screen.
 * Use the touchscreen to navigate menus and adjust settings.
